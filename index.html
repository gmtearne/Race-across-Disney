General rules

This page explains how the game works in general. Each host chooses their own settings, so the exact rules for transport, internet use, penalties and special challenges are shown here once you join a race.

Objective

Complete checkpoints according to your race mode (Speed Race vs Scavenger Hunt). Your final adjusted score or time determines your ranking.

Transportation

Your host decides which transportation is allowed, anywhere from walking only to free park transit, paid options like taxis, or any mode at all. The rules for your race will appear here once you join.

Checkpoint Order & Final Location

Checkpoints can be completed in any order you choose, unless your host has designated a specific ending location for a Speed Race. If a final ending location is chosen, it must be completed last so everyone finishes together.

Penalties

In a Speed Race, skipping a required checkpoint adds a time penalty to your final score. Your host sets the size of the penalty (60 minutes unless they change it). In a Scavenger Hunt there are no time penalties, but skipped checkpoints earn no points.

Bonus Challenges

Bonus checkpoints are entirely optional and will improve your overall standings. There are no penalties for missing bonus checkpoints.

Special Challenges

Depending on your host's settings, a race may include extras such as trivia questions, surprise wildcard bonuses, checkpoint frenzies and Hidden Mickey Bingo. If your race uses them, their rules appear on this page after you join.

Route Planning & Internet Use

Your host decides whether internet help is allowed: none at all, maps and the park app only, or anything goes. The rules for your race will appear here once you join.

GPS Verification & Discrepancies

When checking in, your device GPS will verify your proximity to the location. If you attempt a check-in but your GPS registers you out of the zone, your entry will be flagged as an override and logged for host review.

One surprise bonus offered to every team at a random moment

Adds a brand-new bonus challenge (on top of any bonuses you chose) from a park where the race already has stops. The first team to submit a photo wins it.
