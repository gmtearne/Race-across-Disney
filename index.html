Race Rules & Guidelines

Read carefully before starting your journey across Disney property!

Objective

The primary goal is to complete all assigned mandatory checkpoints as quickly as possible. Your final adjusted time determines your ranking.

Transportation

You can only use free Disney transportation. Absolutely no ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) or paid taxis. Permitted options include buses, the Disney Skyliner, monorails, and watercraft/boats.

Checkpoint Order & Final Location

Checkpoints can be completed in any order you choose, unless your organiser has designated a specific ending location. If a final ending location is chosen, it must be completed last so everyone finishes together.

Penalties

All missed checkpoints will carry a heavy 60-minute time penalty added to your final score. Choose wisely if you decide to skip any stop.

Bonus Challenges

Bonus checkpoints are entirely optional and will improve your overall time via deductions. There are no penalties for missing bonus checkpoints.

Route Planning Restriction

Any use of the internet or external web search tools to look up transit timetables or plan routes during the race is strictly prohibited. Rely on physical park layouts and signage!

GPS Verification & Discrepancies

When checking in, your device GPS will verify your proximity to the location. If you attempt a check-in but your GPS registers you out of the zone, your entry will be flagged as an override and logged for organizer review.

Tip: Always take a photo on your phone's camera roll for your own reference if you believe you are physically standing in the correct location but GPS signal is weak.

Live Leaderboard

Displays raw elapsed time during the race. Official adjusted times (penalties & bonuses) apply upon finishing.
