Strategic Planning Tip

Race Rules & Guidelines

Read carefully before starting your journey across Disney property!

Objective

Complete checkpoints according to your race mode (Speed Race vs Scavenger Hunt). Your final adjusted score or time determines your ranking.

Transportation

You can only use free Disney transportation. Absolutely no ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) or paid taxis. Permitted options include buses, the Disney Skyliner, monorails, and watercraft/boats.

Checkpoint Order & Final Location

Checkpoints can be completed in any order you choose, unless your organiser has designated a specific ending location for a Speed Race. If a final ending location is chosen, it must be completed last so everyone finishes together.

Penalties

All missed checkpoints in a Speed Race will carry a heavy 60-minute time penalty added to your final score. Choose wisely if you decide to skip any stop.

Bonus Challenges

Bonus checkpoints are entirely optional and will improve your overall standings. There are no penalties for missing bonus checkpoints.

Route Planning Restriction

Any use of the internet or external web search tools to look up transit timetables or plan routes during the race is strictly prohibited. Rely on physical park layouts and signage!

GPS Verification & Discrepancies

When checking in, your device GPS will verify your proximity to the location. If you attempt a check-in but your GPS registers you out of the zone, your entry will be flagged as an override and logged for organizer review.
